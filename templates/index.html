Attendance Rules

Attendance Policy

Simple rules that keep our records accurate, fair, and easy to trust.

  1. 1

    Attendance should be submitted within 7 days of the class.

  2. 2

    Same class and date cannot be submitted twice.

  3. 3

    Only an admin can mark attendance on behalf of an absent teacher.

  4. 4

    Holidays are not counted as absent or present.

  5. 5

    Each student can have up to 5 leaves per month.

  6. 6

    Corrections require a reason and are saved in the record.

  7. 7

    If attendance falls below 80%, the student is flagged in red.
